Applicants connect Discord, fill in their in-game name and pick the games they're joining for. Their application lands in a Discord channel, where admins can accept or decline it.

- The Discord connection starts only when someone clicks Apply.
- Admins manage the game list from WordPress, and games map to Discord roles.

Logged & Loaded started as Backlogged: a game tracker and review site for my community and anyone else who wants it.

- Sign in with Discord, then log games as playing, completed or dropped, including repeat playthroughs.
- Every member gets their own profile page with their reviews and collection.
- Game data comes from IGDB.
- A Discord bot keeps the server in sync with new reviews and my live status.
